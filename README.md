# Bike Sharing Demand Explorer

An interactive Streamlit app for exploring hourly bike rentals in Washington, D.C. from 2011–2012.

**Live app:** [Bike Sharing Demand Explorer](https://bike-sharing-system-app.streamlit.app/)

The **Overview** page shows rental summary metrics and charts for demand by hour, day type, season, month, weather, and rider type. Sidebar filters let you select years, seasons, day types, and weather conditions; you can also choose to view a preview of the raw data. The **Conclusion** page summarizes the main patterns and limitations.

## Main conclusions

- Working-day rentals peak around the morning and evening commute hours, while non-working-day demand is highest around midday.
- Average hourly demand is highest in summer and lowest in winter, and was higher in 2012 than in 2011 across months and seasons.
- Rentals tend to be lower in worse weather. Severe-weather observations are scarce, so that comparison is uncertain.
- Registered riders account for more rentals on average than casual riders.

These are patterns in the observed data, not evidence that these factors caused changes in demand.
